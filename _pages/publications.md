## 2026

### Auditing Quality Filters for Long-Tail Human Data Curation

Rishav Agarwal, **Nirshal Chandra Sekar**, Anirudh Vemula

*IROS 2026 Workshop on Long-Term Perception for Human-Centric Autonomy*

We examine how automated data curation can disproportionately discard kneeling and bending workers, and use simulation to investigate detection failures for these poses. The findings highlight the need to preserve underrepresented human behaviors in perception datasets for robots operating around people.
